Computer vision

FocusBubble — real-time focus detector

Python · Flask · OpenCV · MediaPipe · NumPy

  • Combines gaze direction, head pose, eye openness and posture into one live focus score, smoothed with a 5-frame moving average
  • A calibration mode builds a personal baseline in five seconds, so the score adapts to how each person normally sits and looks at the screen
  • Streams the camera feed and the live focus percentage through a small Flask web UI, with an alert when focus stays under 40% for 10+ seconds
  • Optional CSV logging for reviewing focus trends after a work session
